Private inference, side by side

Recorded replay · 5× speed

Two parties classify the same four photos with ResNet-18. Each tile appears when that image’s secure inference finishes.

Timing, image credits and downloads

The two protocols ran separately on the same AMD EPYC 9J14 host. Both clocks start at zero in the replay. The four inferences total 297.74 seconds for Cheetah and 38.40 seconds for OptiPrime. Tile labels show the model’s predictions. Each completion includes online inference through secure argmax and any per-image preprocessing. Model setup, key exchange and opening the outputs for verification are outside the timers. The replay uses the per-image measurements at one fifth of their recorded duration.

Cheetah uses online IKNP and a 60-bit work ring. OptiPrime uses OptiEncode with compact ciphertext output, preprocessed Ferret OT and a 20-bit work ring. Both use the same quantized weights and exact nonlinear operators. These four photos illustrate latency. Classification accuracy is evaluated separately on ImageNetV2.

What the paper shows

Sending encrypted convolution outputs can dominate private inference latency. OptiEncode packs output channels into fewer ciphertexts. OptiPrime combines this encoding with weight compression and a dataflow that reuses ciphertexts on the accelerator.
