Paper overview

Private neural network inference combines homomorphic encryption with multi-party computation. Accelerating the encrypted arithmetic leaves a second cost: transmitting the output ciphertexts. OptiEncode packs convolution output channels into fewer ciphertexts. OptiPrime pairs this encoding with plaintext weight compression and a hardware dataflow that reuses intermediate ciphertexts.

The paper reports up to 5.7× speedup on CPUs and 4.2× with an HE accelerator over Cheetah. The video above measures the SEC-PPDL software implementation. Read the paper evaluation.
